`bark.ogg` is the one bark from the original, trimmed to 0.73 s, faded at both ends, and loudness-normalized. The original is in `assets-src/audio/bark-piecesofuk.ogg`.

The animal calls in `public/assets/audio/wild/` are the clearest calls cut from each recording: high-passed to drop wind rumble, faded, and loudness-normalized. The originals are in `assets-src/audio/wild/`.

The breathing in `public/assets/audio/breath/` comes from the pack's tired, fast, faster, and shivering breathing and its sniffs. The three panting takes are trimmed and crossfaded end to start so they loop, the shivering take is cut into four breaths, and all are loudness-normalized. The originals are in `assets-src/audio/breath/`.

The crickets in `public/assets/audio/forest/` are stretches of each recording, high-passed, loudness-normalized, and crossfaded end to start so they loop. The bird calls are the clearest calls cut from each recording: high-passed, faded, and loudness-normalized. The originals are in `assets-src/audio/forest/`.
