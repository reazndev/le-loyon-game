# Reference: the CLI game

An earlier school project (module M426, by Mateo Muic and Florian Ruby) told the same story as a C# text adventure. The new game takes ideas from it but is not a port.

## What it had

- **Locations:** Village, Church, Graveyard, Forest, Bunker, picked from an ASCII map with number keys.
- **Inventory:** a shovel from the farmer and a military key from the grave.
- **Help forum:** forum posts with hints. Users Kamikaze, Cloudy, Billy, kjhhjahsf, and pivex point the player to the graveyard, the bunker, and the church.
- **Village NPCs:** the farmer lends a shovel. The blacksmith says Le Loyon was seen staring at a grave.
- **Priest:** tells the stories of the three men buried in the cemetery: David Thompson, Michael Roberts, and John Anderson. Ten graves in total; the other seven are filler stories, such as a healer who died in the 1887 plague.
- **Grave digging:** digging the wrong grave triggers a warning. Digging a second wrong one gets the player arrested, which ends the game. Roberts's coffin is empty and holds the bunker key.
- **Chase:** on the way to the graveyard the player sees a figure at the edge of the forest, follows it, and finds the bunker with a locked door.

## Keep for the remake

- The phone forum as hint system and quest log.
- Digging at night with the risk of getting caught. This gives the game a threat without combat.
- The figure seen at the edge of vision, leading into the forest.
- The empty grave as the central reveal.
