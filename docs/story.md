# Story

Plan as of 2026-10-30. The user's first draft (from DMs and the CLI game) was a suggestion, not a script. This file holds the current plan. Items marked _proposal_ are Claude's suggestions that the user hasn't confirmed.

## Decided

- **Name.** The legend is called **Le Loyon**.
- **Village.** **Leurs**, an invented village in the canton of Fribourg. The game never uses a real place name for the village.
- **Era.** November 1998. November fog over the Fribourg plateau is real, so the fog needs no explanation.
- **Player.** A local news reporter from somewhere else in Switzerland. The game never says where. The player character has no name. People call them "you", "the stranger", or "the journalist", so the player feels they are the one there.
- **Prologue.** The game opens with the player browsing early web forums at home, where they first read about Le Loyon. Claude designs these pages in the style of the late-90s web.
- **Names.** All characters get authentic Swiss French names (see below).
- **Truth about Le Loyon.** A real man, about 40, who fought in the Bosnian War and came back with PTSD. He wears the mask and hides because he does not want to be seen. Nothing supernatural.
- **Grave digging.** Digging a grave is a crime. Getting caught ends the game.
- **Endings.** The player is a reporter, so the last choice is whether to publish: keep his secret, publish the story, or leave without finding him.
- **Forum during the game.** The inn has a computer with a dial-up modem. The player checks the forum there.
- **Single player.** Co-op was decided on 2026-10-30 and dropped the same day to keep the game simpler.

## Premise

Late-90s forums carry scattered posts about a man in a gas mask and a long camouflage cape seen in the woods near Leurs. He never speaks, never threatens anyone, and keeps returning to the cemetery on the hill. The villagers call him Le Loyon and treat him as a joke or a myth. The player, a reporter looking for a story, travels to Leurs to find out who he is.

## Structure

1. **Prologue, at home.** The player reads forum threads, a personal homepage with blurry photos, and a guestbook. They note the village name and leave.
2. **Arrival.** The player arrives by train and PostBus. Late afternoon, fog.
3. **Village.** Go house to house. Villagers treat Le Loyon as a myth. The priest tells the stories of the people buried in the cemetery.
4. **First sighting.** On the way to the cemetery the player sees a figure at the edge of their view. Following it leads through the forest to an old army bunker, where a door is locked.
5. **Cemetery.** Three graves matter. The key to the bunker's locked room is in the empty grave.
6. **Bunker.** The player finds Le Loyon and hears his story.
7. **Ending.** Open, see below.

## The three graves

| Grave | What the priest says | Truth |
|---|---|---|
| Daniel Menoud | A gentle, troubled man who took his own life. | Buried there. |
| Jean-Pierre Gremaud | The respected syndic, who vanished without a trace. The family put up a memorial stone. | No body. He was never found. |
| Marc Chassot | From an old village family. Declared dead abroad in the war. Lately, whispers say he came back with a ruined face and wears a mask. | He is alive and is Le Loyon. His coffin is empty and holds the bunker key. |

Because Le Loyon is about 40 and the game is set in the late 1990s, his "death" is recent, only a few years old. The villagers who whisper about him knew him.

## Open story questions

- **How Marc ended up in Bosnia.** Decided: the Bosnian War. Switzerland stayed out, and Swiss law forbids its citizens to serve in foreign armies, which gives Marc a second reason to hide. _Proposal:_ he joined the French Foreign Legion at about 20, under a false identity as the Legion allowed, and served with French UN troops in Bosnia. That explains how he was reported dead under the wrong name. These details need fact-checking before we write dialogue.
- **Why he visits the graves.** _Proposal:_ Daniel Menoud was his childhood friend. The syndic Gremaud knew Marc was alive and helped him hide before he vanished. That gives each grave a reason to exist and a second mystery for the player.

## Ideas still under discussion

Claude's proposals. None are accepted yet.

1. **Day and night.** Daytime in the village is safe. Nights in the cemetery, the forest, and the bunker are not.
2. **Who catches you.** A villager with a flashlight and a dog walks past the cemetery at night. If they see the player digging, they call the police.
3. **Le Loyon as a watcher.** He never attacks. He stands at the tree line, behind a gravestone, at the end of the path, and is gone when the player turns back. Tank controls make turning slow.
4. **The forum reacts.** New posts appear as the player makes progress, and one user always seems to know where the player is.
5. **Camera.** The reporter carries a film camera. The photos are evidence and matter in the ending. Some show things the player didn't see when they took them.
